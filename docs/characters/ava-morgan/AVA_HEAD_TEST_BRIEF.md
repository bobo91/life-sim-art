## Scope and supplied references

Create one manually sculpted, static Ava Morgan head and primary hair forms in Blender, adapting **a fresh working copy of the unmodified Blender Studio Rain v3 source**. Preserve the original separately. Do not use any rejected Ava model. Agents supply the verified source, [locked B reference](AVA_B_REFERENCE_LOCK.json) and existing A03 camera/light template after approval.

The approved **AI-generated** B sheet is the fixed identity, not a request for new designs. Neutral front/profile/three-quarter control form; the small smile is expression reference only. Ava is an adult woman, 29: elongated face, soft defined cheekbones, coherent jaw/chin, narrow brown almond eyes, natural brows, straighter nasal bridge, restrained lips and subtle freckles. Match A03's soft stylization without photoreal pores or doll proportions.

Artist work: sculpt eyes/sockets/lids, cheekbones, nose, mouth and jaw as connected three-dimensional volumes, especially the nose–lip–chin profile. Preserve Rain's working neck/shoulder anatomy. Create brunette hair with her anatomical-left part, a low loose bun, directional flow and a few thin framing locks. Editable mesh/curve hair forms, with a path to game mesh, rather than a required proprietary groom. No new rig, animation, finished body, clothes or hand work.

## Deliverables and acceptance

- Editable, self-contained `.blend`: named head/eyes/brows/hair components, working geometry and sculpt/modifier state where applicable. Provide the editable mesh even if another sculpting tool is used. No required paid add-on or external groom; list tools/versions and dependencies. Existing source rig/scripts are excluded from the static delivery; agents handle safe extraction without executing embedded code.
- Actual Blender neutral front, true profile and three-quarter renders using the supplied A03 light, scale and camera setup; clay face views and simple hair colours. Side-by-side with locked B and read-only A03. No generated replacement images or beauty lighting that hides form.
- Brief change note, component triangle counts and proposed head/hair allowance within the **final whole NPC cap of 30,000 triangles**, leaving room for body/clothing. A working high-resolution sculpt is allowed; final retopology and export are outside this test. Do not sacrifice face forms for premature reduction.
- **One consolidated correction round included**, addressing the owner's three-view feedback within the agreed scope. Acceptance checks B identity in all views, relaxed neutral lids, coherent profile, adult proportions and readable bun/flow. Stop for visual GO/NO-GO after this test; further work is a separate order.
