# B01 bind feasibility notes - source-to-approved-A03 mapping

Read-only source and transformation audit for the owner-authorized Phase B proof. This is not a rigging result or a claim of animation compatibility. A03 identity and the accepted 29,367-triangle mesh remain unchanged.

## Actual transformation chain

1. A01 opens the SHA-pinned Snow source with embedded scripts disabled. It evaluates the source modifier stack at frame 1, with source subdivision set to 0 and Smile.L/R set to .12. The script also assigns lid/corner control offsets. Identical before/after translation logging alone was inconclusive: the actual B01 correspondence test failed by 6.707mm on the head when those assignments were omitted, then passed below 0.000001m when the exact A01 recipe was restored. They must therefore be reproduced.
2. Evaluated mesh coordinates are frozen in source world space, unused vertices are removed, then one shared normalization is applied. The body source has 5,828 raw vertices; its masked evaluated surface has 3,268 referenced vertices after 111 loose vertices are removed. Raw-source vertex indices are therefore not the delivered body's indices.
3. A02 artist-directed fields operate on the frozen A01 metre frame. Head/eye/brow topology is retained; the hair is replaced except for its 547-vertex source scalp cap. Body, shirt, pants and shoes remain unchanged geometrically.
4. A03 adds small approved facial/hair refinement, then simplification of body/clothes/shoes. Head/eyes/brows/hair are not simplified. A03 high preserves the body/clothes/shoes geometry; the final optimized topology is different. Both are static sources without an original armature, keys or vertex-group definitions.

## Actual read-only Blender mapping check

Blender 4.5.14, source auto-execution disabled, no original saved. Final check uses A01 delivery-002, SHA256 8124cc0ef646300ffbc756793582c3a1e4749051cd36d9dd4738ec08a5ee87db.

The normalized mapping is approximately:

`p_A01 = (p_source_world - (-0.0000004172325, -0.0525042898953, -0.00344038009644)) * 0.9725384948534507`

The offset is recovered from the unchanged body bounds; float rounding leaves maximum source-to-A01 nearest residuals of 0.000000134m for body, 0.000000241m for shirt, 0.000000182m for pants and about 0.000000131m for shoes. Head/eyes residuals are about 0.000000124m. The eyebrows differ intentionally after the A01 raise, up to 0.749mm. All referenced evaluated source vertices in those parts retain weight memberships. These measurements establish a usable coordinate correspondence, not that source rest and evaluated bone frames match.

The local ignored evidence is `build/male-neutral-b01/mapping-audit-002/mapping-report.json`, generated by the adjacent read-only inspection script. An earlier inspection used an intermediate equal-geometry A01 source; the final -002 check uses the correct SHA-pinned delivery.

## Smallest credible binding proof

- Establish whether the source deform-bone rest matrices match the evaluated T-pose. If not, build the new bind from the corresponding evaluated bone frames and explicitly validate the resulting neutral mesh. Do not silently combine a baked posed surface with unrelated raw bind matrices.
- Preserve approved final A03 vertex positions, material/UV data and triangle count. Treat them as the new neutral bind surface; do not round-trip the face through original Snow Basis.
- Recover skin weights from the normalized **evaluated** Snow surfaces, retaining group names. For unchanged high body/clothes/shoes, an exact-position mapping is possible. Transfer to simplified output with same-part nearest-triangle barycentric interpolation and normalize deform-only groups. Validate gaps between clothing and arms, both sides of thin shoe pieces, left/right assignment, zero-weight vertices and maximum influences.
- On the head, retain the neck/spine blend. The head mesh includes the neck to about z1.305m; rigidly parenting the entire mesh to a head bone may separate it from the collar. The original 3,719-vertex head correspondence can recover these memberships. For a locomotion-only proof, facial descendant groups can collapse to the head carrier while retaining neck weights; eyes/brows/hair may follow the head rigidly. This deliberately does not prove facial animation.
- Reuse only the necessary source deform hierarchy or a small derived locomotion skeleton. Do not deliver the hundreds of control/helpers or assume CloudRig constraints/drivers export to Unity. Preserve attribution for the source skeleton and weights.
- Test relaxed arms, shoulders, elbow/wrist/finger flexion, hip/knee/ankle poses, then bounded idle/walk/run/turn clips. Inspect actual rendered deformation before calling it game-ready.

## Remaining risks and limited alternative

A03 was evaluated with source corrective smoothing, masks and keys; exporting only linear skinning does not automatically reproduce the original deformation quality. The source rig may use segmented twist/bendy chains and face/lattice dependencies that cannot become a small Unity Humanoid skeleton by deleting control bones alone. Original facial keys use old facial proportions and domains; retaining their values against an altered Basis can corrupt A03 identity.

If full Snow reuse is unsuitable, stop that path and present a bounded **body-only deform skeleton/weight-transfer** proof using the approved static A03 and frozen face. Do not escalate into a large facial-key or universal clothing-system rebuild. A future clothing pipeline needs hidden torso/leg geometry (A03 only delivers visible masked skin), matching bind pose, consistent cloth/body deformation and explicit garment coverage. Future identity/body variants need weight/shape transport checked per changed topology; original weight indices cannot be copied onto optimized meshes.
